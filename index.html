The idea

Social apps took the world and flattened it into a feed. DSCO puts it back on the map.

A live map of your world

Friends and familiar faces around you in real time. No feed, no doomscroll — just who's actually near.

Friends, made in person

Meet someone at a show or a park — find them on the map and keep them. Chats form around real places.

Moments on the corner

Stories, pins, and notes live where they happened — not in an algorithm's void.

Privacy

Location is the product, so consent is the architecture.

You choose who can see you — everyone, friends only, or no one. Going invisible takes one tap. Blocking erases you from each other's worlds. Nothing about your location is ever sold.
